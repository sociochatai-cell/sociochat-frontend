FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Is SocioChat the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. SocioChat is built on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) from Meta. Your account is properly registered, so broadcasts and automations do not risk a ban like they do on the free WhatsApp Business app.

What's included in the ₹1,499/mo launch price?

The full Pro plan: unlimited contacts & broadcasts, multi‑agent inbox, AI chatbot, drip & retargeting, WhatsApp catalog, payments in chat (UPI/cards/netbanking), interactive flows & bookings, Shopify/WooCommerce/Razorpay integrations, and full analytics. WhatsApp conversation charges are billed by Meta directly at their published rates.

How long does it take to go live?

Most businesses go live within 30 minutes — connect your number, verify the business with Meta, load a template and send. Our team helps with Blue Tick verification if you want it.

Do I need a developer to use it?

No. The whole platform is designed for business teams: broadcasts, chatbot, catalog, drip and payments are all no‑code. Developers can still use our REST API and webhooks when they want to.

Can I import my existing contacts?

Yes — upload a CSV or connect a Google Sheet. Contacts can be tagged, segmented and used across broadcasts, drip and the AI chatbot.

What about the free WhatsApp Business app I already use?

The free app is great for 1:1 chats, but it wasn't built for marketing or automation. Broadcasts on the free app are capped at ~256 contacts and marketing sends can get your number banned. SocioChat runs on the official API, so you can broadcast at scale, automate replies, take payments and stay compliant.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, cancel anytime — you keep access until the end of the billed period.
